How to play

  1. Walk by tapping or holding your finger on the map.
  2. Train in the Finger Gym. Real finger exercises make your fighter bigger.
  3. Fight street fighters for coins, and follow the red arrow to the Stadium to fight bosses.
  4. In a fight, tap PUNCH fast and hold BLOCK when you see the red !
  5. Spend coins in the Shop on gear.
