Finger Fighter

Privacy policy · Last updated 7 October 2026

In short: Finger Fighter does not collect, store or share any of your personal information. There are no accounts and no ads.

Your game progress

Your progress (coins, levels, gear and settings) is saved only on your own device, using your browser's storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else. If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, your progress is deleted.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We don't use analytics or tracking.

Ads and purchases

The game has no ads and no in-app purchases. Coins in the game are earned by playing and have no real-money value.

Fonts

The game loads its lettering from Google Fonts. To send the font, Google's server receives a normal web request from your device (which includes your IP address). The game itself does not send any of your information with it. You can read Google's privacy policy.

Children

Finger Fighter can be played by children. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Use the developer email shown on Finger Fighter's Google Play page.
